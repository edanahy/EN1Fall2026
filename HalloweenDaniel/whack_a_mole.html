How to play

  • Start main.py on the hub from the REPL (e.g. mpremote run main.py). Put the car in the middle of the table between the players, facing Player 1, and click Connect car.
  • Player 1 whacks with Q W E, Player 2 with I O P, one key per hole.
  • Whack a mole first: the car drives one step toward you. Whack an empty hole: it drives one step away from you. A mole someone already whacked doesn't count for anyone.
  • Pull the car all the way to your side to win. Press Space to start and Esc to stop. A new game drives the car back to the middle first.
